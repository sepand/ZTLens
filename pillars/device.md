# Pillar 2: Device

Establish and maintain trust in every device (managed, unmanaged, IoT, OT)
before and during its access to enterprise resources.

## Key principles

- Every device has a real-time trust score, not a one-time approval.
- Compliance and health posture are continuously verified, not checked only at enrollment.
- Unmanaged and IoT/OT devices are inventoried and constrained even if they can't run an agent.

## Maturity snapshot

| Stage | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Traditional | Manual asset inventory, periodic compliance audits |
| Advanced | MDM/EDR-driven inventory and compliance scoring, posture checks gate access |
| Optimal | Hardware-rooted attestation, continuous real-time device trust scoring |

# Pillar 7: Visibility & Analytics

Provide visibility into user, device, application, and network activity so
that relevant context can inform policy decisions and threat detection across
every other pillar.

## Key principles

- You can't enforce policy on signals you don't collect.
- Logging and monitoring are centralized, correlated, and continuously analyzed — not siloed per system.
- Analytics feed back into automation and policy decisions in near real time.

## Maturity snapshot

| Stage | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Traditional | Siloed logs, manual review |
| Advanced | Centralized SIEM with correlation rules across most systems; automated threat intel ingestion |
| Optimal | Behavioral analytics (UEBA) with automated risk scoring feeding policy decisions in real time |

# Pillar 6: Automation & Orchestration

Automate manual security processes to take policy-based, risk-informed
actions across the enterprise with speed and at scale.

## Key principles

- Policy decisions are centralized (a Policy Decision Point) and enforced consistently everywhere (Policy Enforcement Points).
- Response to detected risk is automated wherever confidence allows, freeing analysts for judgment calls.
- Orchestration ties signals from every other pillar into a single enforcement fabric.

## Maturity snapshot

| Stage | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Traditional | Manual incident response runbooks, policy enforced locally per system |
| Advanced | Automated SOAR playbooks for common alerts; centralized PDP/PEP for major application tiers |
| Optimal | Self-healing, policy-driven response integrated across all pillars; unified PDP evaluating real-time cross-pillar risk |

# Pillar 4: Data

Data is the asset Zero Trust ultimately protects. This pillar focuses on
transparency and visibility enabled by enterprise infrastructure, applications,
standards, robust end-to-end encryption, and data tagging/classification.

## Key principles

- Data is classified and labeled before access policy can be applied to it.
- Encryption is applied by default, not as an exception.
- Access to data is governed by policy tied to sensitivity, not just to the system that stores it.

## Maturity snapshot

| Stage | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Traditional | Manual, inconsistent classification; encryption applied ad hoc |
| Advanced | Automated DLP/CASB-driven classification; policy-enforced encryption across most stores |
| Optimal | Continuous ML-assisted classification driving real-time access policy; end-to-end encryption with centralized key management |
